## 2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on the Services and features you use.

### 2.1 Account and authentication information

When you sign in with Google, we receive the information authorized by the Google sign-in flow and returned by Google. This may include:

- Your Google account identifier.
- Your email address and whether Google has verified it.
- Your name and profile picture, where provided by Google and used by the implementation.
- Authentication identifiers needed to create and maintain your Nerdmaxxing account and session.

We also collect your Nerdmaxxing username, if you choose one, and the account and session information needed to authenticate you. We do not collect every field in your Google profile merely because it exists; the information we receive depends on the permissions and fields requested by the implementation.

### 2.2 Profile, social, and challenge information

Depending on the features you use, we collect:

- Your username, display name, profile photo, biography, and profile settings.
- Follower and following relationships.
- Squad or group memberships, join requests, and invitations.
- Challenge participation, progress logs, completion status, milestones, achievements, skills, aura points, streaks, and leaderboard information.
- Comments, replies, reports, notifications, and other interactions with social features.
- Preferences such as whether your profile is private or whether you receive push notifications.

Information you choose to put in a public profile, public challenge, public activity, leaderboard, group, comment, or other public feature may be visible to other users. You control some visibility settings through the Services, but no online service can prevent others from copying information that they can see.

### 2.3 Challenge evidence

Some challenges let you submit evidence of an activity. Evidence may include text, explanations, external URLs, images, video, documents, or other files, together with metadata such as the associated challenge, participant, submission time, file name, file type, file size, review status, verification result, and rejection reason.

Evidence submitted for verification is stored privately and is not publicly displayed by default. It may be accessed by you, authorized reviewers, administrators where necessary, and service providers acting on our behalf to store or process it. Please do not include unnecessary personal information or information about another person in an evidence submission.

If a future feature makes evidence visible to other users, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain any consent required by law before using it for that purpose.

### 2.4 External account verification

Some challenges can verify activity through a connected third-party account, such as Chess.com. When you use that feature, we may collect the provider, provider account identifier or username, connection status, verification code or proof, public profile information used for verification, and the verified metric or observation returned by the provider.

The current Chess.com connection flow uses public profile information and a temporary verification code. It is not an OAuth login and does not collect your Chess.com password. We do not accept client-supplied ratings as proof. Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies.

### 2.5 Technical, security, and diagnostic information

When you use the Services, we and our service providers may automatically process information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot them, including:

- IP address, request metadata, timestamps, and server logs.
- Device, operating system, browser, application version, and platform information.
- Error details, crash information, performance information, and diagnostic events where enabled.
- Authentication, abuse-prevention, rate-limiting, and security events.

We do not use technical information to infer sensitive characteristics about you. We will update this Policy if our collection practices materially change.

### 2.6 Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, we collect an app/device registration token, platform, and token activity information so that our notification provider can deliver notifications. You can disable push notifications through your device or application settings. A token may remain in provider or device logs for a limited period after deactivation, subject to those providers' practices.

### 2.7 Information you send us

If you contact us, we collect the email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide so that we can respond, provide support, or handle a privacy request.

## 4. What is public and what is private?

Information intended to be visible to other users may include your username and selected profile details, public challenge activity, achievements, completed public challenges, aura points, leaderboard position, follower or following relationships, public group participation, and public comments or replies.

Your email address, authentication credentials, session tokens, private profile information, private challenge information, and challenge evidence are not intended to be publicly displayed merely because you use the Services. Evidence submitted for manual review remains private by default.

Visibility can differ by feature and may change when you change a setting or use a new feature. Information shared publicly may be copied, captured, or redistributed by others. Removing information from Nerdmaxxing may not remove copies that others have independently retained.
