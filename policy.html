2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on the Services and features you use.

2.1 Account and authentication information

When you sign in with Google, we receive information authorized by the Google sign-in flow and returned by Google. This may include your Google account identifier, email address and verification status, name and profile picture where provided, and authentication identifiers needed to create and maintain your account and session.

We also collect your Nerdmaxxing username, if you choose one, and account and session information needed to authenticate you. We do not collect every field in your Google profile merely because it exists.

2.2 Profile, social, and challenge information

Depending on the features you use, we collect your username, display name, profile photo, biography, profile settings, follower and following relationships, squad or group memberships, join requests, invitations, challenge participation, progress logs, completion status, milestones, achievements, skills, aura points, streaks, leaderboard information, comments, replies, reports, notifications, and other social interactions.

Information you choose to put in a public profile, public challenge, public activity, leaderboard, group, comment, or other public feature may be visible to other users. You control some visibility settings through the Services, but no online service can prevent others from copying information they can see.

2.3 Challenge evidence

Evidence may include text, explanations, external URLs, images, video, documents, or other files, together with the associated challenge, participant, submission time, file name, file type, file size, review status, verification result, and rejection reason.

Evidence submitted for verification is stored privately and is not publicly displayed by default. It may be accessed by you, authorized reviewers, administrators where necessary, and service providers acting on our behalf. Please do not include unnecessary personal information or information about another person in an evidence submission.

2.4 External account verification

Some challenges can verify activity through a connected third-party account, such as Chess.com. We may collect the provider, provider account identifier or username, connection status, verification code or proof, public profile information used for verification, and the verified metric returned by the provider.

The current Chess.com connection flow uses public profile information and a temporary verification code. It is not an OAuth login and does not collect your Chess.com password. We do not accept client-supplied ratings as proof.

2.5 Technical, security, and diagnostic information

We and our service providers may process IP address, request metadata, timestamps, server logs, device and operating system information, browser and application version, error details, crash and performance information where enabled, and authentication, abuse-prevention, rate-limiting, and security events.

2.6 Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, we collect an app or device registration token, platform, and token activity information so that our notification provider can deliver notifications. You can disable push notifications through your device or application settings.

2.7 Information you send us

If you contact us, we collect the email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide so that we can respond, provide support, or handle a privacy request.
